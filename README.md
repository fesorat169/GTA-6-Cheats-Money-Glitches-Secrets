## About this project

GTA VI returns to Vice City and the state of Leonida. This repository collects **cheat codes, unlock requirements, money-making strategies, and gameplay discoveries** in one readable place. It's intended to evolve as more players test the game.

What we track:

- **Cheat codes:** Controller inputs, effects, and unlock conditions.
- **Money strategies:** Robberies, vehicles, fences, banking, and other legitimate in-game methods.
- **Glitch reports:** Reproducible bugs with evidence and clear platform/version details.
- **Corrections:** Broken codes, patched exploits, and differences between game versions.

**Platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. An official PC release date had not been announced as of the update above.

## How to activate GTA 6 cheats

Early-access guides describe this process **after completing the main story and satisfying the specific cheat's unlock requirement**:

1. Press **↑ on the D-pad** to bring up the phone.
2. Hold **L1** (PS5) or **LB** (Xbox).
3. Press **R1** (PS5) or **RB** (Xbox).
4. Enter the cheat's directional combination.
5. Check **Progress → Cheats** to review available unlocks.

> ⚠️ **Save first.** Published guides report that activating cheats disables saving and trophies/achievements for the current session. The $250,000 cheat is therefore **not** a permanent money-farming method.

## Money glitches: verification status

**No permanent unlimited-money glitch is currently verified in this repository.**

A **cheat code** is an intended game feature. A **money glitch** is unintended behavior. A **money-making method** is normal gameplay. They are not the same thing.

| Method | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $250,000 cheat | **Reported by guides** | Requires story completion; cheat sessions cannot be saved |
| Vehicle duplication | **Unverified** | No reproducible, save-persistent evidence accepted |
| Infinite resale / fence payout | **Unverified** | No reproducible, save-persistent evidence accepted |
| Repeated mission payout exploit | **Unverified** | No reproducible, save-persistent evidence accepted |

A genuine exploit report should show the **starting balance**, **exact actions**, **resulting balance**, and **whether the extra money survives a save and reload**. Reports that only display a large balance are not enough.

## Best ways to earn money

Until reliable glitches are documented, the safest starting point is ordinary gameplay:

**1. Steal and sell valuable vehicles.** Check vehicle information and resale opportunities before stealing. Avoid unnecessary damage on the way to a buyer.

**2. Rob businesses carefully.** Stores and other properties can offer cash and valuables. Look for additional rooms or goods before leaving.

**3. Explore criminal hideouts.** Gang operations and other locations may contain cash or items that can be sold.

**4. Find fences for stolen goods.** Not all loot instantly becomes spendable money. Some valuables must be sold to the appropriate buyer.

**5. Protect physical cash.** Early gameplay reporting describes a distinction between carried cash and banked funds. Deposit money when banking becomes available, and avoid carrying more than you need.

These are **gameplay tips, not glitches**. Their profitability may change as players test routes, payouts, and updates.
